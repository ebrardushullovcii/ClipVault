One key. Saves the last 2 minutes.

ClipVault keeps a rolling replay buffer while you play. Press your save hotkey and the last two minutes are saved as an MP4 file on your PC, with game audio and your microphone on separate tracks.

Seg 01

The Play

Three steps from the moment to the file.

  1. Cam 1

    01Play

    ClipVault runs in the tray with an always-on replay buffer. By default it holds the last 2 minutes. It can start with Windows.

  2. Hotkey

    Clip savedLast 2:00 · MP4

    Cam 2

    02Press your hotkey

    The save hotkey (F9 by default, changeable in Settings) uses a low-level keyboard hook, so it works while a fullscreen game has focus. Windows confirms with a “Clip Saved” notification.

  3. 03It’s in the library

    The clip is at the top of your library right away: a normal MP4 named with the date and the game, like 2026-09-28_23-30-00_VALORANT.mp4.

Seg 02

Analysis

The editor on the clip we just saved. Trim it, deal with the mic, pick a size.

  1. Iso 1

    1Trim the play

    Drag the two trim handles. Playback stays inside the trim, and frame-step and 5-second skip help you find the exact moment. This clip: 0:38 to 1:04, 25.8 seconds.

  2. Iso 2

    2Mic on its own track

    Game/desktop audio and your microphone are recorded to separate tracks. Turn either one off or change its volume before you export. Here the mic is off.

  3. Iso 3

    3Up to 10 MB

    Pick a size target: Up to 10 MB (Discord-friendly), 50 MB, 100 MB, or keep original. H.264 is the most compatible. AV1 gives better quality per MB and needs an NVIDIA RTX 40-series or newer GPU.

After the whistle

Export done. Drag it anywhere.

After export, a small window lets you drag the file into any app, copy its path or open its folder. “Trim Original” trims the file in place instead. In the editor you can also add tags, favorite a clip, change its game tag, and export at 30 or 60 fps and 720p or 1080p.

Seg 05

Rulebook

How ClipVault records, and what it stays out of.

  1. Rule 01

    Monitor capture is the default

    ClipVault records your monitor with DXGI capture. You choose which monitor in Settings.

  2. Rule 02

    No game hooks. No injection.

    Monitor capture does not hook into games or inject anything into them. That keeps it anti-cheat friendly.

  3. Rule 03 Under review · Experimental

    Game-only recording is optional

    “Games automatically” records supported games only and follows the active game. “Monitor + automatic games” is also available. Both are experimental. You can also pick an app window by hand.

  4. Rule 04

    Your clips stay on your PC

    Clips are ordinary MP4 files in a folder you choose. No account. Nothing is uploaded.

  5. Rule 05

    GPU first, CPU as backup

    NVENC on an NVIDIA GPU for the lowest CPU use, with x264 on the CPU as the fallback. “Auto” picks. Record in H.264 or AV1.

Seg 07

Post-Game Q&A

Straight answers.

QIs ClipVault free?
A

Yes. It is free and open source under GPL-2.0-or-later, and it does not need an account. The source code is on GitHub.

QWhat do I need to run it?
A

A 64-bit PC with Windows 10 or 11. There is no macOS or Linux version. With an NVIDIA GPU it records with NVENC for the lowest CPU use; otherwise it falls back to the x264 CPU encoder.

QDoes it hook into my games?
A

Not in the default mode. ClipVault records your monitor with DXGI capture, with no game hooks or injection, which keeps it anti-cheat friendly. The optional “Games automatically” mode is marked experimental.

QCan I change the save hotkey or the 2-minute length?
A

Yes. The save hotkey is F9 by default, changeable in Settings. The 2-minute buffer length is adjustable there too. The first-run setup also walks you through the clips folder, capture, audio and hotkey.

QWill my voice be in every clip?
A

Your microphone is recorded to its own track, separate from game audio. In the editor you can turn either track off or change its volume before you export.

QHow do I get a clip small enough for Discord?
A

Choose “Up to 10 MB” when you export. You can also pick 50 MB, 100 MB or keep the original, choose H.264 or AV1, and set 30 or 60 fps and 720p or 1080p.

QWhere are my clips stored?
A

In a folder you choose, as ordinary MP4 files named with the date and the game. Nothing is uploaded.
