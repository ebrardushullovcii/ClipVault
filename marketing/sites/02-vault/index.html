01 · Box room

Safe-deposit boxes

Eight boxes, one feature in each. Hover, tap or tab to a box to pull it out and see it in the app.

Box No. 001 · Audio

Game audio and mic on separate tracks

Desktop and game audio go on one track, your microphone on another. In the editor you can turn either track off or change its volume before you export.

  • 2 audio tracks
  • Mute or adjust per track

Box No. 002 · Library

Clips play when you hover them

Every clip gets a thumbnail. Hover a card and the clip plays right in the grid. Switch between grid and list view, search, and sort by date, size, name or favorite.

  • Grid or list
  • Search and sort

Box No. 003 · Library

Every clip tagged with its game

Detected games are tagged automatically, and the game is in the file name too, like 2026-09-28_23-30-00_VALORANT.mp4. Filter the library by game, by tag, or to favorites only.

  • Game
  • Tag
  • Favorites

Box No. 004 · Library

Work on many clips at once

Select several clips, then favorite, tag, add or remove a game, export or delete them together.

  • Favorite
  • Tag
  • Game
  • Export
  • Delete

Box No. 005 · Editor

Trim to the moment

Drag the trim handles and playback stays inside the trim. Frame-step and 5-second skip controls help you place the cut. Export the trimmed part, or use Trim Original to trim the file in place.

  • Frame step
  • 5 s skip
  • Trim Original

Box No. 006 · Export

Export under 10 MB for Discord

Pick a size target when you export: up to 10 MB, 50 MB or 100 MB, or keep the original. You can also set 30 or 60 fps and 720p or 1080p. Afterwards, a small window lets you drag the file anywhere, copy its path or open its folder.

  • 10 / 50 / 100 MB
  • 30 / 60 fps
  • 720p / 1080p

Box No. 007 · Codecs

H.264 or AV1

H.264 is the most compatible choice. AV1 gives better quality per MB and needs an NVIDIA RTX 40-series or newer GPU. Pick the codec for recording in Settings and for each export in the editor.

  • H.264 · most compatible
  • AV1 · RTX 40-series+

Box No. 008 · Encoding

NVENC, with x264 as the fallback

With an NVIDIA GPU, ClipVault encodes with NVENC for the lowest CPU use. Without one, it uses the x264 CPU encoder; Auto picks for you. Record at 30, 60, 120 or 144 fps with one of five quality presets, from Compact to Maximum.

  • Auto / NVENC / x264
  • Up to 144 fps

03 · Holdings

Held on your PC

ClipVault is a local app. Your clips are files on your own disk, and they stay there.

  1. I

    Nothing is uploaded

    Clips are saved to your disk. ClipVault does not upload them anywhere.

  2. II

    No account

    Nothing to sign up for. A first-run setup walks through the clips folder, capture, audio and hotkey, and you are ready.

  3. III

    Plain MP4 files, in a folder you pick

    Every clip is an ordinary MP4 file. Copy it, back it up or open it in another program, like any other file.

  4. IV

    No game hooks, no injection

    By default ClipVault records your monitor with DXGI capture. It does not hook into or inject anything into the game, which makes it anti-cheat friendly. Automatic game capture (experimental) and single-window capture are optional.

05 · Plaques

Questions, answered

I

What does ClipVault cost?

Nothing. It is free and open source under GPL-2.0-or-later, and the source code is on GitHub.

II

Which Windows versions?

Windows 10 and Windows 11, 64-bit. The installer is ClipVault-Setup-1.7.6.exe.

III

Does it hook into my games?

No. The default capture records your monitor with DXGI, with no game hooks or injection, which keeps it anti-cheat friendly.

IV

Can I change the hotkey or the 2 minutes?

Yes. The save hotkey is F9 by default, changeable in Settings, and the buffer length (2 minutes by default) is adjustable there too. The hotkey works while a fullscreen game has focus.

V

Where are my clips saved?

In the folder you choose during setup, as plain MP4 files. Nothing is uploaded and there is no account.

VI

Do I need an NVIDIA graphics card?

No. With one, ClipVault uses NVENC for the lowest CPU use; without one, it uses the x264 CPU encoder. AV1 needs an NVIDIA RTX 40-series or newer.
