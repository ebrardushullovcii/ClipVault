01 / SaveHotkey → Clip Saved → Library

Save

One key. The last 2 minutes become a file.

The replay buffer is always running. The save hotkey uses a low-level keyboard hook, so it works while a fullscreen game has focus. Windows shows “Clip Saved” and the clip is at the top of your library right away.

04 / CleanMonitor capture · Local files · No account

Clean

No game hooks. No injection. No account.

A

Monitor capture

The default. ClipVault records your monitor with DXGI capture. It does not hook or inject into the game, which keeps it anti-cheat friendly.

B

Game-only mode Experimental

“Games automatically” records supported games only and follows the active game. You can also pick one app window by hand.

C

Local files

Clips are ordinary MP4 files in a folder you choose. There is no account, and nothing is uploaded.

06 / FAQShort answers

FAQ

  1. 01

    Is it free?

    Yes. ClipVault is free and open source under GPL-2.0-or-later. There is no account to create.

  2. 02

    Which Windows versions?

    Windows 10 and Windows 11, 64-bit. The installer is ClipVault-Setup-1.7.6.exe.

  3. 03

    Does the hotkey work in fullscreen games?

    Yes. It uses a low-level keyboard hook, so it works while a fullscreen game has focus. The save key is F9 by default, changeable in Settings.

  4. 04

    Can I keep more than 2 minutes?

    2 minutes is the default buffer length. You can change it in Settings, and trim any clip shorter in the editor.

  5. 05

    Does it hook into my games?

    The default monitor capture (DXGI) uses no game hooks or injection. Game-only recording is an optional, experimental mode.

  6. 06

    Where do my clips go?

    Into a folder you choose, as ordinary MP4 files named with the date and the game. Nothing is uploaded.

  7. 07

    Can I remove my mic from a clip?

    Yes. Game audio and your mic are recorded to separate tracks. In the editor you can turn either one off or change its volume before export.

  8. 08

    Will a clip fit in Discord?

    Export with the “Up to 10 MB” target. There are also 50 MB and 100 MB targets, or you can keep the original.

  9. 09

    What does it use to encode?

    NVENC on an NVIDIA GPU for the lowest CPU use, or x264 on the CPU. “Auto” picks one. AV1 needs an RTX 40-series or newer GPU.
