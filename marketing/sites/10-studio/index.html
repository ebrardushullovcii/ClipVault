How it works

From the moment to the file.

What happens after you press your hotkey, one step at a time.

  1. 01 · Library

    Saved, and already in your library.

    Windows shows a “Clip Saved” notification and the clip appears at the top of the library. Detected games are tagged automatically, and the file name includes the game, for example 2026-09-28_23-30-00_VALORANT.mp4.

  2. 02 · Preview

    Hover to preview.

    Clips play in the grid when you hover over them. Switch between grid and list view, search by name, and sort by date, size, name or favorite.

  3. 03 · Trim

    Trim to the moment.

    Drag the trim handles and playback stays inside the selection. Frame-step and 5-second skip controls help you place each cut. Here, a two-minute clip becomes 25.8 seconds around the round win.

  4. 04 · Audio

    Game and mic, on separate tracks.

    Desktop audio and microphone are recorded to their own tracks. Turn either one off, or change its volume, before you export.

  5. 05 · Size

    Pick a size, then export.

    Choose Up to 10 MB for Discord, 50 MB, 100 MB, or keep the original. Export as H.264 for the widest compatibility, or AV1 for better quality per MB on an NVIDIA RTX 40-series or newer GPU.

  6. 06 · Share

    Drag it wherever it goes.

    When the export finishes, a small window opens with the new file. Drag it anywhere, copy its path, or open its folder.

Organizing, encoding, and running quietly in the background.

Separate game and mic tracks.

Game or desktop audio and your microphone are kept on two tracks in every clip. Drop your voice from a clip, or turn the game up, without re-recording anything.

Questions and answers

Is ClipVault free?

Yes. ClipVault is free and open source under GPL-2.0-or-later. The source code is on GitHub.

Which versions of Windows does it support?

Windows 10 and Windows 11, 64-bit (x64).

Does the save key work while I’m in a fullscreen game?

Yes. The hotkey uses a low-level keyboard hook, so it works while a fullscreen game has focus. You can change the key in Settings.

How long are the clips?

By default your hotkey saves the last 2 minutes. You can change the buffer length in Settings, and trim any clip in the editor.

Does ClipVault hook into my games?

Not in the default mode. Monitor capture (DXGI) records the screen and uses no game hooks or injection, which keeps it friendly to anti-cheat. The “Games automatically” modes are optional and experimental.

Where are my clips stored?

As ordinary MP4 files in a folder you choose. There is no account, and nothing is uploaded.

Can I make a clip small enough for Discord?

Yes. Export with the “Up to 10 MB” size target. You can also pick 50 MB, 100 MB, or keep the original.

Do I need an NVIDIA graphics card?

No. ClipVault uses NVENC on NVIDIA GPUs for the lowest CPU use and falls back to x264 on the CPU. AV1 needs an NVIDIA RTX 40-series or newer GPU; H.264 works everywhere.

Can I remove my voice from a clip?

Yes. The microphone is recorded on its own track. Turn it off in the editor before you export, and the game audio stays.

Keep your next great play.

Install ClipVault, play as usual, and press your hotkey when a play is worth keeping.
